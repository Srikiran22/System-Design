## How to use this map

1. Identify the domain and applicable jurisdiction.
2. Determine whether the system is safety-, mission-, security-, privacy- or financially critical.
3. Locate the standards owner/regulator, then check the latest edition and adoption status.
4. Map requirements to architecture and assurance evidence.
5. Identify cross-domain standards that interact (e.g. functional safety plus cybersecurity plus quality management).
6. Engage domain-qualified safety, legal, compliance and certification experts early.

A domain standard is a scoped obligation or accepted practice within its field; it is not a universal architecture template.
