## What this repository is

A structured knowledge base for understanding a problem as a system, designing a solution, justifying it, operating it, and improving it as evidence changes. It is not a list of fashionable technologies or a universal architecture recipe.

It connects three related disciplines:

- **System design / software architecture:** translate stakeholder and product needs into components, interfaces, data, deployment, controls, and operational behavior.
- **Systems thinking:** understand the wider system of people, incentives, information, resources, feedback, delays, constraints, and unintended consequences in which a product operates.
- **Systems engineering:** manage the lifecycle from stakeholder needs and requirements through architecture, implementation, integration, verification, validation, operation, maintenance, and disposal. It applies to software, hardware, infrastructure, and socio-technical products.

## The core design loop

1. **Frame the outcome:** who needs what result, and what business or mission goal is expected?
2. **Bound the system:** users, operators, dependencies, data, external actors, and exclusions.
3. **Discover constraints:** budget, time, skills, laws, legacy, platforms, data residency, latency, operational capacity.
4. **Make requirements testable:** functional behavior plus prioritized quality attributes and measurable scenarios.
5. **Model the system:** domain boundaries, data, flows, interfaces, deployment, trust boundaries, failure domains, people/organization.
6. **Create alternatives:** at least two plausible designs plus a simple baseline.
7. **Evaluate trade-offs:** requirements, risks, assumptions, sensitivity points, lifecycle cost.
8. **Prove risky assumptions:** prototypes, load tests, fault injection, security tests, user validation.
9. **Operate and learn:** telemetry, SLOs, recovery drills, incident learning.
10. **Revisit:** change architecture when evidence or context invalidates assumptions.

## What “exhaustive” means here

No finite search can guarantee every concept, paper, certification, implementation, or incident has been found. This is a broad, structured research repository from multi-angle discovery and primary-source extraction, expanded with systems science, architecture evaluation, requirements, distributed/cloud/data systems, safety, security, V&V, MBSE, enterprise architecture, AI, human factors, careers, tooling, control/CPS, operations research, integration, logistics and sustainability. The later passes extend this into mission engineering, methodological pluralism, formal methods, system-theoretic safety, agentic AI security, probabilistic risk, regulatory assurance, model/simulation credibility, lifecycle V&V, environmental qualification, safety-case argumentation, sector assurance and quantitative reliability statistics. See 79 and DEEP_SCRAPE_AUDIT.csv for extraction status; see DEEP_COVERAGE_MATRIX.csv for remaining gaps. It records coverage and limitations and gives a repeatable expansion method; it does not claim that the global field has been completely crawled.

Sources are referenced as `[S01]` etc. Resolve IDs in [18 — Source library](18_SOURCE_LIBRARY.md) or `sources.csv`. Vendor frameworks are authoritative for their own frameworks, not neutral proof that a vendor product is universally best. Company blogs and postmortems are first-hand but context-specific. Research preprints are not settled standards.
